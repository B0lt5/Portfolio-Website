How we work

A clear process.
No guesswork.

You should know what happens next at every stage. Here's the approach I use to keep projects collaborative and focused.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We discuss your business, your audience, the problem to solve, and what a successful outcome should look like.

  2. 02

    Planning

    I define the project scope, proposed features, timeline, and price so we understand what will be delivered.

  3. 03

    Development

    I build the website, test its functionality, and keep you informed for feedback at the right points along the way.

  4. 04

    Launch

    We review the completed work, address agreed revisions, and prepare the website for launch and handover.

Good to know

Questions you
might have.

A few basics to help you decide whether we could be a good fit.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the size and requirements of the project. Once we discuss your goals, I'll outline the scope and agree on a price with you before any work begins.

How long will my project take?

Timelines vary with the scope, content, and feedback involved. I'll provide a realistic estimate as part of the plan before development starts.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can look at what is and isn't working, then agree on the improvements that best support your goals and your visitors.

What if I'm not sure what I need yet?

That's completely fine. Get in touch and we can talk through your idea, audience, and priorities before deciding what to build.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes. We can discuss the project remotely and keep in touch throughout with clear updates and review points.
